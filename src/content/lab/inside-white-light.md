**Input:** a webcam pointed at a desk lamp, used as the "white light" source.

**Process:** a GLSL TOP splits the image into a dozen wavelength bands and offsets each one with a slightly different refraction amount, so the lamp fans out into a spectrum. Moving the lamp moves the rainbow.

**What we noticed:** the edges of everything in the room were quietly hiding rainbows.
