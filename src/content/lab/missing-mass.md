**Process:** Niagara particle galaxy driven by the real rotation speeds. The gap between observed and expected speed becomes a faint halo of dark particles, and a post-process pass bends the background stars around it like gravitational lensing.

**What we noticed:** the invisible part is most of the picture — the halo is several times bigger than the galaxy.
