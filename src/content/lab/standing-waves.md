**Input:** a sine-wave sweep from 80 Hz to 1 kHz, plus a voice humming along.

**Process:** a digital Chladni plate. Particles drift toward the still points of a vibrating surface, and the pitch from an Audio Analysis CHOP picks which pattern they settle into. Higher notes give more intricate shapes.

**What we noticed:** some notes make clean geometry and some never settle — the in-between pitches are the most interesting.
